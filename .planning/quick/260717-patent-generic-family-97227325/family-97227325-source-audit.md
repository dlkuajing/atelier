features configurable for different imaging requirements. It publishes no ordered radius,
thickness, material, refractive-index, Abbe, or asphere rows, and no exact EFL or F-number.

zoom narrative; three “visual angle” phrase matches (two singular, one plural) belong to the
fifth-embodiment vehicle coverage range. Neither is embodiment-specific prescription metadata. Therefore the five disclosed
embodiments are source-locked confirmed-no-prescription architecture terminals, and no worker,
request, receipt, fingerprint, candidate, or ZMX may be created.
