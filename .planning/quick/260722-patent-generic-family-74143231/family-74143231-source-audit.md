## Prescription boundary

- Paragraph 56 and claims publish `0.3<R/D<12` and `0.5<R/D<10`. Paragraphs 83,
  98, 117, 123, 129 and 136 publish six `R`, `D` and `R/D` observations. Here `R`
  is a spherical support curvature radius and `D` the minimum mechanical distance
  from that support to the reflecting surface. These are image-stabilization
  support geometries, not ordered optical surface coordinates.
- Paragraph 60 says at least one plastic-prism entrance/exit surface *can* be
  optical-aspheric, paragraph 106 identifies both such surfaces in item 3, and
  claim 15 repeats the generic option. No radius, conic or coefficient is supplied.
- Paragraph 4's single focal-length occurrence describes conventional telephoto
  systems. Paragraphs 142 and 146 provide only qualitative deployment fields; no
  numeric angular field is bound to an imaging-lens prescription.
- The exact source publishes no ordered optical radius/surface sequence,
  intersurface spacing, numerical optical material/index/dispersion row,
  surface-specific conic/asphere coefficient, identified aperture-stop coordinate,
  focal length, F-number, numeric angular field or absolute image height.
- Drawings are not measured and their geometry is not promoted to a prescription.
- Therefore items 1-6 close as
  `confirmed_no_prescription.reflection_image_stabilization_architecture_only` and
  items 7-8 close as
  `confirmed_no_prescription.electronic_device_wrapper_only`.
