## Prescription boundary

Tables 1A, 2A, and 3A publish only outer-diameter stripe and molding geometry: angles,
stripe lengths, optical-region distances, center thickness, and their mechanical ratios.

no ordered radii, axial spacings, optical materials, refractive indices, Abbe numbers,
asphere coefficients, stop position, EFL, F-number, optical field, or image height.
Accordingly, none of the first seven items is an optical prescription.

examples, and digital zoom behavior. Paragraphs 121-124 disclose vehicle camera placement
and a vehicle coverage angle. That visual angle describes scene coverage for camera
placement; it is not reclassified as a lens angular-field prescription. These three
wrappers add no ordered prescription. All ten source items are therefore exact
`confirmed_no_prescription` terminals, with no conversion request.
