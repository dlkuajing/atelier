the ordered surface radii, axial spacings, numerical material/index/dispersion
rows, aperture-stop row, conics/asphere coefficients and remaining required EFL,
F-number and absolute image-height fields occur only in the printed table
figures. The HTML contains zero machine-readable table rows.

Two independently downloaded 3,061,567-byte official PDF containers have

eight optical/asphere table pairs are pages 6/7 through 34/35 at four-page

34-40 and 51-53 were reviewed only to establish page and table-category

No OCR, image enhancement, repair, drawing measurement, raster numeric
transcription or raster numeric inference was used. Project evidence policy
does not authorize converting printed raster values into formal optical data.
