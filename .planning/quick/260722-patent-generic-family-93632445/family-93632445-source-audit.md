The source directly publishes laser wavelengths, polarizations, optical-power
relationships, chromaticity coordinates, speckle contrast, beam splitters and
combiners, phosphors, filters, modulators, reflectors, color-filter wheels and
projection-system/wrapper architecture. “Lens group” and “lens assembly” are
generic functional components. Paragraph 120 says only that a lens assembly has
one or more convex or concave lenses and images a modulated beam; paragraphs 125
and 127 only say a lens group collimates a beam.

No source item or claim publishes:

- an ordered optical surface-radius or curvature sequence;
- ordered axial intersurface spacings;
- numerical optical material, refractive-index or dispersion rows;
- a surface-specific conic constant or asphere coefficient;
- an identified aperture-stop coordinate;
- focal length or F-number;
- a numeric prescription-bound angular field; or
- absolute image height.

The two `field of view` occurrences describe the driver's front view in the HUD
wrapper and are not numeric lens fields. Claims 1–20 add source, system and method
architecture but no separate prescription.

`confirmed_no_prescription` terminals: four light-source architectures, three
projection-system architectures, four projection-light-beam transmission
methods, one projection method and five device/system wrappers. No worker,
conversion request, receipt, fingerprint, ZMX, formal intake or CODE V use is
