## Adjudication

The only required system field not directly published is an exact F-number. Detailed
paragraph 16 and claim 8 state only the system-wide upper bound `F-number <= 1.8`.
The directly published focal lengths and entrance-pupil diameters are not divided to
derive F-number. Both source items therefore terminate precisely as
`metadata_unpublished.system_f_number_absent`; no conversion request, receipt,
prescription fingerprint, candidate ZMX or formal intake is permitted.

Source typography is preserved rather than repaired. In particular, the normalized
HTML retains the printed/OCR tokens in the Abbe header and Table 12 condition row, and
the negative-ratio inequality ordering is not rewritten. Those auxiliary tokens do not
supply the missing exact F-number and are not used to create prescription values.
