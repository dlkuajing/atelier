time-division multiplexing, displays, absorbers, polarizers, mirrors or stray-light
control. They do not publish a complete item-specific sequence of free-form
surfaces, local frames, optical material and exact system F-number. These 27 items
are terminal `confirmed_no_prescription.freeform_tir_display_architecture_only`.

Item 22, paragraphs `[0154]` through `[0161]` and `FIGS. 32-34`, is numerical. It
publishes five tenth-order Legendre free-form surfaces, their domains and
coefficients, six local origins, 4 mm pupil performance, about 26 mm front focal
length, 108° horizontal and 93° vertical full fields, and a 2.1-inch/55 mm 16:9
display. It publishes no prescription-specific optical material, refractive index,
Abbe number, wavelength or exact system F-number. The sole exact `F-number` token
belongs to the prior-art discussion in paragraph `[0029]`. Consequently item 22 is
terminal
`metadata_unpublished.required_optical_material_and_exact_system_f_number_absent`.

The 4 mm pupil and approximate 26 mm focal length are not converted into an inferred
F/#. Drawing coordinates are not measured. The non-sequential RXIR/TIR/reflection
path is not flattened into the repository's rotational sequential ZMX contract.
