## Numeric examples and terminal boundary

The disclosure's numeric examples were reconciled rather than treated as missing table rows:

- one experiment uses an imaging lens with `F=90 mm`, aperture `D=16 mm`, and object/detector
  distances to demonstrate the phase element; it publishes no lens radii, thicknesses, glass,
  index/Abbe data, stop location, asphere coefficients, or surface sequence;
- the human-eye experiment states that its optical parameters follow an external publication,
  but does not reproduce a surface prescription;
- `2.6 mm` ring diameter, about `3.5 mm` replication period, `350 nm` etch depth, and `2 mm`
  flat-glass width describe phase-pattern fabrication, not a sequential imaging-lens
  prescription;
- the seven `aspheric` occurrences describe cited prior art, not a disclosed surface table.

Consequently, the complete document is one architecture/experiment item, not a collection of
formal optical prescriptions. Each exact source terminates as
`confirmed_no_prescription.extended_depth_of_focus_phase_element_architecture_only`; no worker,
conversion receipt, prescription fingerprint, or ZMX is created.
