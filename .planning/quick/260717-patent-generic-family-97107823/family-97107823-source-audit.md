## Optical boundary

- Examples 1-7 of the 1st Embodiment vary tapered light-blocking-sheet surfaces, layer stacks,
  coatings, materials/glossiness and mechanical sheet thickness. Examples 1-2 of the 2nd
  Embodiment add anti-bending-sheet and overlapping-sheet configurations.
- Numeric disclosures are mechanical aperture values: `T=0.035/0.05/0.06 mm`,
  `T=0.023 mm`, `S=0.049 mm`, `T/S=0.469`, `Ti=0.033 mm`, `Tii=0.05 mm` and
  `Ti/Tii=0.66`, plus explicitly bounded sheet ratios. They are not lens-center thicknesses.
- The specification names generic lens elements, their order along an optical axis, a barrel,
  filter, image sensor and an object-side adjustable aperture. It publishes no lens count fixed by
  an example, ordered radius/spacing/material row, aspheric coefficient, glass/index/Abbe value,
  EFL, F-number/FNO or field/FOV metadata.
- Paragraph 0140 says “Tables show different data” as boilerplate, but the HTML has no table block,
  the official PDF has no table page, and every PDF page was visually inspected. No drawing value
  is derived or promoted to an optical prescription.
